For Katie: This is a first-contact prototype, not a reading assessment or mastery check. It introduces uppercase M and lowercase m with optional freehand tracing; tracing is not evaluated for stroke order or accuracy. Letter-name and visual recognition only. Teaching the /m/ sound requires a clear, educator-reviewed recording; it is not taught in this version. Please evaluate the teaching, accessibility, pacing, feedback, and whether the letter choice and progression are instructionally appropriate. Prerecorded narration plays when audio clips are installed; device speech remains a temporary fallback.
