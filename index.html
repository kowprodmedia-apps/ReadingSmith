For Katie: This first adventure introduces uppercase and lowercase M, then practices visual identification. It does not yet teach the /m/ phoneme, because browser text-to-speech is not reliable for isolated sounds. It is NOT a placement test or an approved phonics sequence. Please critique the clarity, teaching method, feedback and age-appropriateness. The animated fairy companion is an interaction/character prototype; its dialogue still uses your device's text-to-speech and is not a finished character performance. Phoneme-level audio is intentionally not synthesized; educator-reviewed recorded phonics audio is needed before child testing.
